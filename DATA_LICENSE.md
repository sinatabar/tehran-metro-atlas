# Data and attribution

**Spatial database.** The scoped Geofabrik/OpenStreetMap inputs, normalized OSM objects, background geography, source-way index, derived exports and public use of the combined atlas database carry © OpenStreetMap contributors attribution under the Open Database License 1.0. The project offers the machine-readable derivatives, reviewed inputs and transformation scripts free of charge in this repository. Keep attribution and the license link with copies, maps and extracts. Apply share-alike to publicly used adaptations as required by the license. [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) · [OSMF attribution guidelines](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines)

**Independent research.** Contributors license their original factual compilation, editorial notes and translations separately under CC BY 4.0 to the extent they hold rights. Credit “Tehran Metro Atlas contributors”, identify the release and describe modifications. Individual facts may carry no copyright. This grant covers the project's own contributions only. The research sidecar crosswalk includes OSM identifiers for reference. For the combined machine-readable database the project also offers its own database rights under ODbL 1.0. Separate directories preserve the origin and standing of each input. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [OSMF horizontal layers guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Community_Guidelines/Horizontal_Map_Layers_-_Guideline)

Operator schematics, news articles, photographs, government drawings and the historical Google My Maps content remain subject to their owners' rights. This release publishes independently worded observations, bibliographic references and OSM geography. It contains no copied Google geometries, passenger-map artwork or raster tile archives.
